\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The headline ladder is on SOCOFing, whose probes are synthetic same-capture degradations rather than
independent acquisitions; the PolyU/FVC2002 results confirm this is optimistic (cross-sensor transfer
sits at chance). The accuracy gain is gap-dependent (Theorem~\ref{thm:relaxation}): it recovers
discriminability lost to quantization but cannot manufacture any the extractor lacks, so it is dramatic
on SOCOFing, a wash or regression where the 16-D embedding is already near the float ceiling, and
should not be extrapolated to settings with no gap. Communication is a trade-off, not a win: the $45\%$
narrows our own gap to Blind-Touch's $856$\,KB compressed CKKS rather than closing it, bought by
eliminating the $117$\,MB Galois key and packing ceiling and for a 16\,B template. Security is
semi-honest only (a malicious client or input substitution is out of scope, and the Bui--Cong
backend~\cite{BuiCong25} is likewise semi-honest), the feature-head map is not claimed one-way, and
at-rest confidentiality against a compromised server is out of scope. The feature head is trained with
a non-convex straight-through estimator, so we claim no global optimum, only that training reliably
moves the impostor law toward the uniform ideal.
