\section{Simulators}
\label{app:hybrids}

We give the two simulators of \S\ref{sec:security} at the deployed operating point $(w,t,T)=(6,3,18)$;
the full hybrid distinguisher reductions summing to~\eqref{eq:bound} are in the extended version.

\paragraph{Corrupt client.} $\mathsf{Sim}_{\mathsf{C}}$ receives $\xt$ with
$(\mathsf{out}, \mathcal{L}_{\mathsf{C}})$ and produces the mOPRF outputs, the sender-side silent-VOLE
transcript, and the polynomials $\{\hat z_i\}$: sample $r_j \leftarrow \mathbb{F}$ for $j \in [T]$ as
the mOPRF outputs; for each $i \in \mathsf{out}$ sample a label $\hat\ell'_i$ and a uniform
label-sharing polynomial $\hat f_i$ of degree $\le \theta-1$ with $\hat f_i(0)=\hat\ell'_i$; build
$\hat z_i$ by setting $\hat z_i(x_j)=\hat f_i(x_j)$ at $j \in S_i$ (read from
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathsf{C}}$), sampling uniformly at the other interpolation points, and interpolating
the unique degree-$\le 2T$ polynomial; emit the sender correlation messages as uniform vectors. Every
value not pinned by $\mathcal{L}_{\mathsf{C}}$ is uniform, matching the real view.

\paragraph{Corrupt server.} $\mathsf{Sim}_{\mathsf{S}}$ outputs $\bot$, so its only task is the
transcript. The single input-dependent message $\Delta U = u_0 - u'_0$ is \emph{not} uniform on
$\mathbb{F}^{2T+1}$ but lies in the $(T{+}1)$-dimensional image of the degree-$\le T$ evaluation map,
so the simulator samples a uniform degree-$\le T$ polynomial and outputs its evaluation vector, and
simulates the silent-VOLE receiver messages with the base-VOLE simulator; since $u'_0$ is uniform,
$u_0-u'_0$ is uniform in that subspace and indistinguishability is statistical.

\paragraph{The masking step.} The load-bearing hybrid replaces each non-colliding term
$v_1(x_j) = f(x_j) + (\prod_i (x_j - \yt_i))\,R(x_j)$ by a uniform element. With $J = \{j : j \notin
S_i\}$, the map $R \mapsto (\prod_i(x_j-\yt_i)\,R(x_j))_{j\in J}$ is a scaled evaluation map, and since
$R$ is uniform of degree $\le T$ its evaluation vector on any $\le T+1$ points is uniform, so the
substitution is perfect provided $|J| \le \deg R + 1$. Here $|J| \le T$ and the implementation sets
$\deg R = T$, so this holds by construction at every operating point, $T=18$ included. Two polynomials
must not be conflated: the label-sharing $f_i$ has degree $\le\theta-1$, while the masking $R$ has
degree $T$ and carries no label. All $N$ instances share one receiver polynomial and one PRF key, so
the PRF and masking hybrids are stated over the whole database under a single union bound, giving the
$N(2T+1)/|\mathbb{F}|$ term.
